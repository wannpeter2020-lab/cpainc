Out of Tolerance — How It Works

For each of Kristin's bookings, the system calculates an expected commission using: Actual Pickup Rooms × Room Rate × Commission % × Split. If no pickup data is available, it falls back to USD Revenue × Commission % × Split.

The payment received is then compared to that expected amount. If it is short by more than {{ (tolerance * 100) | round(1) }}%, the row is flagged Underpaid and highlighted in red.

The {{ (tolerance * 100) | round(1) }}% threshold accounts for small rounding differences in currency conversions and rate calculations — it only fires when a payment is meaningfully short. This threshold can be adjusted in Settings. Out-of-tolerance flags only apply to Kristin's bookings, where expected commission can be calculated from pickup data.
