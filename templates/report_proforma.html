Report Structure & Logic

One Excel workbook covering 2026 through the last meeting year on record. Each year = two tabs (Kristin / Team).

Blue/White Actual commission payment received — amount exactly as recorded. Revenue column = Pipeline Revenue.
Yellow Unpaid/projected — commission = 1% of Pipeline Revenue × your adjustment %. Pay date projected using brand/chain avg days.

Rollover logic: if a meeting's projected pay date falls in 2027 or later, it appears on that year's tab automatically.

Days timing: brand 6-month avg → chain 6-month avg → 90-day default.
